Political Planner is an all-in-one digital assistant built to simplify the daily responsibilities of political leaders, personal assistants, coordinators, and party teams. From managing meetings and appointments to tracking expenses, organizing travel, assigning tasks, storing documents, and maintaining a daily diary, everything is available in one secure and easy-to-use platform. Designed for efficiency, the app helps political work stay organized, transparent, and always on schedule.

Events & Appointments

Stay organized by managing political meetings, public events, appointments, and office schedules from one place. Record event details, locations, timings, participants, and notes for easy reference. Keep every commitment well planned and accessible.

Members & Task Assignment

Add team members, volunteers, assistants, and coordinators with ease. Assign responsibilities, distribute tasks, and monitor progress efficiently. Ensure everyone knows their role and stays aligned with campaign or office activities.

Task Calendar

View all assigned tasks and scheduled activities in a clear calendar layout. Plan daily, weekly, and monthly work without missing important deadlines. Keep political operations organized with better visibility and scheduling.

Travel & Itineraries

Plan official visits, campaign tours, constituency travel, and meetings effortlessly. Store travel schedules, destinations, accommodation details, and important notes in one organized itinerary for quick access anytime.

Expense Management

Maintain a complete record of campaign, travel, office, and event expenses. Log income and expenditures with proper details for better financial tracking. Review spending history whenever needed for improved accountability.

Meeting Gallery

Organize photos from meetings, events, campaigns, and official visits. Store visual records alongside meeting details for easy reference. Keep important moments securely documented in one dedicated gallery.

Intelligent Reminders

Receive timely reminders for meetings, appointments, follow-ups, and important political activities. Never miss critical schedules with smart notifications designed to keep your day running smoothly and efficiently.

Political Planner empowers political professionals with smart tools for planning, coordination, communication, and daily administration. Manage meetings, tasks, travel, expenses, documents, and reminders all from one reliable platform. Stay organized, stay productive, and focus on serving your people while Political Planner takes care of the rest.
